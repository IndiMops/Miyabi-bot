# Miyabi Bot Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 24, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Miyabi Bot, operated by the developer known as **IndiMops**, accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when users interact with the bot through Discord.

Miyabi Bot is an unofficial community application and is not operated by Discord or HoYoverse.

## 1. Information Miyabi Bot May Process

When you interact with Miyabi Bot, Discord may provide the bot with information necessary to execute commands and provide its features.

Depending on the command and enabled features, this information may include:

* Discord user ID;
* Discord server ID;
* Discord channel ID;
* Discord role IDs;
* usernames, display names, server names, channel names, or role names;
* command names, command options, and other information intentionally submitted through bot commands;
* server-specific bot settings and preferences;
* language or localization preferences;
* timestamps associated with commands or stored records;
* virtual account, profile, inventory, statistics, or progression data if role-playing or progression features are enabled;
* technical error information and diagnostic logs.

Discord IDs are numerical identifiers assigned by Discord. They may be used to associate settings or bot features with the correct user, server, channel, or role.

## 2. Message Content

Miyabi Bot does not collect the contents of unrelated Discord conversations merely because the bot is present in a server.

The bot may temporarily process message content when:

* a user directly invokes a message-based command;
* a feature specifically requires analysis of a message;
* Discord provides the content as part of an interaction initiated by a user;
* processing is necessary to moderate abuse, diagnose an error, or protect the service.

Message content is not sold or used for advertising.

Where possible, application commands and interaction data are used instead of continuously monitoring messages.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

* respond to commands;
* provide game-related information;
* maintain server-specific configuration;
* remember user or server preferences;
* provide localization;
* operate virtual progression or role-playing systems;
* prevent spam, abuse, fraud, or unauthorized use;
* diagnose errors and improve reliability;
* secure and maintain the bot;
* comply with legal obligations and Discord’s applicable policies.

Information will not be used for purposes that are materially incompatible with those described in this Policy.

## 4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the European Union General Data Protection Regulation applies, information may be processed based on:

* performance of a service requested by the user;
* the legitimate interests of operating, securing, and improving the bot;
* compliance with legal obligations;
* consent, where consent is required by applicable law.

Users are not required to provide optional information. However, some commands or features may not work without the information necessary to perform them.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

Some information may be processed only temporarily while a command is being executed.

Server settings, user preferences, virtual progression, and similar information may be retained while they are necessary to provide the corresponding feature.

Diagnostic logs may be retained for a limited period to investigate errors, abuse, or security incidents.

Information may be deleted or anonymized when:

* it is no longer required for the bot’s functionality;
* a server removes the bot and the retained configuration is no longer needed;
* the bot or a specific feature is discontinued;
* Discord requests deletion;
* a valid deletion request is received;
* deletion is required by applicable law.

Backups, if used, may retain deleted information temporarily until they are overwritten during the normal backup lifecycle.

## 6. Data Sharing

Miyabi Bot does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only:

* with Discord as necessary to operate through the Discord platform;
* with infrastructure, hosting, database, monitoring, or other service providers necessary to operate the bot;
* when a user intentionally requests a feature involving a third-party service;
* when required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request;
* when reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users, or secure the service.

Service providers may process information only for the purposes required to provide their services.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Miyabi Bot relies on Discord and may use external APIs or data sources to provide game-related information and other functionality.

Information processed directly by Discord is governed by Discord’s own Privacy Policy.

External websites, APIs, or services linked or used by the bot may have separate privacy practices. Users should review the policies of those services where appropriate.

## 8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect retained information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Users should never submit passwords, authentication tokens, payment information, government identification documents, or other highly sensitive information through bot commands or GitHub issues.

## 9. Children’s Privacy

Miyabi Bot is intended only for users who meet Discord’s minimum age requirements.

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children who are not legally permitted to use Discord. If such information is identified, it may be deleted.

## 10. User Rights and Data Requests

Depending on applicable law, users may have the right to:

* request access to information associated with them;
* request correction of inaccurate information;
* request deletion of stored information;
* object to or restrict certain processing;
* request information about how their data is used;
* withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

A request should include the requester’s Discord user ID and enough information to identify the relevant records.

Do not publicly post private or sensitive information when submitting a request.

The developer may request reasonable verification to ensure that a person requesting access or deletion is the relevant user or an authorized server administrator.

## 11. Server Administrator Requests

A Discord server owner or authorized administrator may request deletion of server configuration associated with their server.

Deletion of server-level data does not necessarily include data independently associated with individual users where retaining it remains lawful and necessary.

## 12. International Data Processing

The bot and its service providers may process information in countries other than the user’s country of residence.

Where required, reasonable measures will be used to protect information transferred internationally.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot’s features, infrastructure, data practices, legal requirements, or Discord policies change.

The date at the top of this document indicates the latest revision.

Material changes may also be announced through the project repository, bot documentation, support server, or relevant bot command.

## 14. Contact and Data Deletion Requests

Privacy questions and requests to access or delete information may be submitted through the Miyabi Bot GitHub repository:

[IndiMops/Miyabi-bot](https://github.com/IndiMops/Miyabi-bot)

When creating a GitHub issue:

1. state that the request concerns privacy or data deletion;
2. provide your Discord user ID or server ID;
3. do not include authentication tokens, passwords, or other sensitive information.

For requests containing private information, ask the developer for a private communication method rather than posting the information publicly.
